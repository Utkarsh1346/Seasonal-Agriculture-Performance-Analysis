# Seasonal-Agriculture-Performance-Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural performance across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons using farm, crop, environmental, resource, production, and economic data.

The project focuses on identifying seasonal patterns, comparing agricultural performance, analyzing profitability and resource usage, and understanding environmental and disease/pest risk factors.

## Objectives

- Analyze agricultural performance across different seasons
- Clean and prepare the agricultural dataset
- Compare crop and state-wise performance
- Analyze yield, production, and profitability
- Study irrigation methods and water efficiency
- Examine environmental factors and disease/pest risk
- Generate meaningful data visualizations and insights

## Dataset

The dataset contains 4,000 records and 28 attributes covering:

- Farm information
- State and district
- Crop and season
- Environmental conditions
- Soil characteristics
- Fertilizer and pesticide usage
- Irrigation methods
- Yield and production
- Market price
- Cost, revenue, and profit
- Water usage and efficiency
- Disease and pest risk

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy

- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Findings

- Kharif recorded the highest total production of 82,387.61 tonnes.
- Kharif recorded the highest average yield of 5.64 tonnes/ha.
- Kharif recorded the highest average profit of ₹1,78,914.65.
- Zaid recorded the lowest average profit of ₹-24,804.82.
- Drip irrigation recorded the highest average profit of approximately ₹2,19,626.
- Sugarcane recorded the highest average profit among the analyzed crops.
- Punjab recorded the highest total production among the analyzed states.
- Kharif recorded the highest average disease and pest risk.
